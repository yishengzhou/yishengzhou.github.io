Hello, I'm Shengzhou Yi

AI Researcher at the University of Tokyo


My research focuses on large language models, multimodal learning, and human-centered AI.
I develop AI systems for understanding, evaluating, and supporting human communication, with applications to online interviews, mental health, presentations, and content generation.
